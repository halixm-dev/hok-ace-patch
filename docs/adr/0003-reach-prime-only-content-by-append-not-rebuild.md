# Reach prime-only content by append, not rebuild

Prime-only assets could reach the phone by a rebuild of their package with Tencent's builder, and the game mounts the result, but rebuilding package `21` makes the Factionless card Yixing draw as the game's grey placeholder, the builder cannot emit the record shapes Tencent's own package uses, and the game's repair cannot converge on a re-laid-out package. So no package is rebuilt (the rebuild list stays empty, `21` in particular) and prime-only content reaches the override layer by append, which adds records and leaves the package's existing shape untouched. Evidence: [prime-only-append.md, Problem and journey log](../features/prime-only-append.md#s1-problem), [prime-rebuild.md, Out of Scope](../features/prime-rebuild.md#s3-out-of-scope) and [lab notebook §8 and §9](../lab-notebook.md#8-why-package-21-is-not-rebuilt).
